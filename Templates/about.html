About DELUX Cars

Founded in 2018 in Bhadrapur, DELUX Cars has grown to become the leading premium car dealership in Koshi Province. We specialize in luxury, performance, and electric vehicles from the world's top brands.

Our mission is simple: Deliver excellence in every drive. With a team of certified technicians and a state-of-the-art showroom, we ensure every customer drives away happy.

Our Values

  • Transparency in pricing
  • Quality assurance on every vehicle
  • Customer-first service
  • Sustainable and eco-friendly options
